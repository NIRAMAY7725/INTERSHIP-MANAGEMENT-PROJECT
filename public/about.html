What is InternManage?

InternManage is a digital solution that connects students, companies, and colleges on a single platform. It helps in posting, applying, tracking, approving, and managing internships in a transparent and efficient way.


Our platform streamlines the entire internship lifecycle — from posting opportunities to issuing completion certificates — making the process hassle-free for everyone involved.
