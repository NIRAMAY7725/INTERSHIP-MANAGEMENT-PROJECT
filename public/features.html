⭐ Our Features

Powerful Features for Seamless
Internship Management

InternManage offers a complete set of features to help students, companies, and institutions manage internships efficiently in one place.

Everything You Need, All in One Place

👥

User Management

Separate dashboards for Students, Companies, and Admins with secure authentication and role-based access.

💼

Internship Listings

Companies can post internship opportunities with details like duration, stipend, skills, and application deadline.

📝

Easy Applications

Students can browse and apply for internships in just a few clicks and track their application status in real-time.

📊

Application Tracking

Track applications at every step — Applied, Shortlisted, Accepted, Rejected — with real-time notifications.

📄

Report Submission

Students can upload their internship reports, which can be reviewed and approved by the admin.

✅

Approvals & Verification

Admins can verify applications, approve reports, and ensure the internship process is smooth and transparent.

🏆

Certificate Management

Approved students can download their completion certificates directly from the platform.

📈

Reports & Analytics

Admins can generate insightful reports and analytics on internships, applications, and student performance.

🔒

Secure & Reliable

We prioritize the security of your data with best practices and encryption.

📱

Responsive Design

Access the platform seamlessly on desktop, tablet, or mobile devices.

🔔

Real-time Notifications

Stay updated with real-time alerts for applications, approvals, and more.

💬

Dedicated Support

Our support team is always ready to help you with any queries.
