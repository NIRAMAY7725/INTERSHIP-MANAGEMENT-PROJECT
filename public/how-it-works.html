How InternManage Works

Our simple and efficient process connects students, companies, and institutions to make internship management hassle-free.

1
👤

Register

Students, companies, and admins register on the platform with their details.

- - →
2
🔍

Browse Opportunities

Students can browse available internships posted by registered companies.

- - →
3
📝

Apply for Internships

Students apply for internships that match their skills and interests.

- - →
4
📋

Review & Shortlist

Companies review applications and shortlist candidates who best fit the role.

- - →
5
⭐

Approve & Assign

Selected students are approved by the company and the admin (if required).

- - →
6
🏆

Complete & Certify

Students complete the internship, submit reports, and receive certificates.

Three Roles, One Platform

👨‍💻

For Students

  • ✅ Create profile & upload resume
  • ✅ Find and apply for internships
  • ✅ Track application status
  • ✅ Submit reports
  • ✅ Download certificates
🚀

For Companies

  • ✅ Post internship opportunities
  • ✅ Review and manage applications
  • ✅ Shortlist and select candidates
  • ✅ Monitor intern progress
  • ✅ Issue completion certificates
🛡️

For Administrators

  • ✅ Manage users and companies
  • ✅ Approve internships and reports
  • ✅ Monitor activities
  • ✅ Generate reports & analytics
  • ✅ Ensure smooth process
