🔒 Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

InternManage is committed to protecting the privacy of students, companies, and institutions using our internship management platform.

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information such as your name, email address, profile details, internship preferences, application data, and communication history when you use our platform.

2. How We Use Information

We use your information to provide internship management services, connect students with companies, support communication, track applications, and improve platform functionality.

3. Security

We use reasonable technology and safeguards to protect data stored on our systems and prevent unauthorized access.

4. Sharing Information

We do not sell personal data. Information may be shared only with authorized stakeholders involved in the internship process, such as approved companies or institutions.

5. Cookies and Analytics

We may use cookies or similar tools to improve user experience, analyze traffic, and understand how features are used.

6. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting our support team.

7. Updates

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the platform after a change indicates your acceptance of the updated policy.
